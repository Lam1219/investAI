How this works

RRSP wins when your marginal tax rate today is higher than your marginal tax rate in retirement. TFSA wins in the opposite case.

Where to find each number

  • CPP: Log into My Service Canada Account → "Statement of Contributions" shows your estimated retirement pension.
  • OAS: Auto-calculated here. Full OAS in 2026 is ~$7,900/yr, reduced proportionally if you lived in Canada less than 40 years after age 18.
  • Workplace pension: Your employer's pension administrator provides a retirement estimate.
  • RRSP/RRIF withdrawals: A common rule of thumb is the 4% rule: withdraw 4% of your portfolio in the first year of retirement, adjusted for inflation.

OAS clawback note

If your taxable retirement income exceeds ~$91,000 (2026), OAS is clawed back at 15¢ per dollar. This effectively raises your marginal rate in that band. This calculator doesn't model the clawback, but if you're near that threshold, consider consulting a planner.

Assumptions

2026 federal + provincial tax brackets (indexed estimates). Does not model CPP/EI premiums, dividend gross-up, capital gains inclusion, or RRSP overcontribution penalties.
